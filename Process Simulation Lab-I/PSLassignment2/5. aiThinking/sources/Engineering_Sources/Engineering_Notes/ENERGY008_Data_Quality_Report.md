## 4. Reaction reference dataset

The dataset stores balanced stoichiometric forms for:

1. CO₂ hydrogenation to methanol: CO₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ CH₃OH(l) + H₂O(l)
2. Reverse water-gas shift: CO₂ + H₂ ⇌ CO + H₂O(g)
3. CO hydrogenation to methanol: CO + 2H₂ ⇌ CH₃OH(l)

Calculated standard reaction enthalpies are:

| Reaction | ΔH° at 298.15 K, 0.1 MPa(a) | Propagated source uncertainty |
|---|---:|---:|
| CO₂ + 3H₂ → CH₃OH(l) + H₂O(l) | −131.820 kJ/mol reaction | ±0.242 kJ/mol |
| CO₂ + H₂ → CO + H₂O(g) | +41.154 kJ/mol reaction | ±0.218 kJ/mol |
| CO + 2H₂ → CH₃OH(l) | −128.970 kJ/mol reaction | ±0.262 kJ/mol |

Uncertainty is propagated by root-sum-square from the listed formation-enthalpy uncertainties, assuming independent inputs. These are **reference-state calculations**, not reactor-temperature heat release. Gas-phase product water, real-gas effects, sensible heat, pressure-dependent enthalpy and phase changes must be treated before process heat duties are calculated.

No reaction rate, conversion, selectivity, equilibrium constant or catalyst model is frozen.

## 6. Correlation and unit safeguards

`scripts/unit_conversions.py` provides explicit conversions for:
- °C ↔ K.
- bar(a) ↔ MPa(a).
- bar(g) → bar(a) only when local atmospheric pressure is supplied.
- kg/h ↔ kg/s.
- kmol/h ↔ mol/s.
- kW ↔ W.
- kWh ↔ MJ and GJ ↔ MJ.
- g/mol ↔ kg/kmol identity conversion.

There is no implicit gauge-to-absolute pressure conversion. Correlation helper functions in `scripts/property_correlations.py` reject temperatures outside the correlation's stated interval. Missing values remain `null`/blank and must be interpreted with the associated status field.
