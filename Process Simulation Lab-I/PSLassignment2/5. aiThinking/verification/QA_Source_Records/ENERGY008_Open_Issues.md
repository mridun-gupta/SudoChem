## Interpretation and sequencing

- **First gate:** close `ISS-001` and `ISS-002` before any design-basis freeze. The site, capacity, product grade, feed envelope and route must be approved, and Route B must be independently evaluated or explicitly recorded as a formally accepted scope deviation.
- **Process model gates:** close `ISS-003` through `ISS-007` before claiming plant balances, equipment sizing, utilities, energy KPIs or an operating window. Current stoichiometric/thermochemical checks are reference calculations only.
- **Design and execution gates:** close `ISS-008` through `ISS-012` with vendor/site inputs and competent engineering reviews. HAZID and preliminary safeguards are not substitutes for formal hazard studies or statutory design review.
- **Software gates:** close `ISS-013` through `ISS-015` and `ISS-017` by running the MATLAB project on a supported licensed runtime, performing GUI/fault tests, comparing MATLAB and Python results, and qualifying target operating systems.
- **Decision and sign-off gates:** close `ISS-016` after a physically valid model and constraints exist; close `ISS-018` through the institute submission process.

## Superseded sensitivity issue

The earlier Prompt 21 sensitivity defect concerning a throughput-only perturbation was superseded by the Prompt 22 release. Prompt 22 now includes 12 independent one-at-a-time studies and an 8-dimensional, 1,000-sample Latin-hypercube screening study with corresponding records and test logs. This closes the sensitivity-method minimum at the **screening level only**; it does not close `ISS-016` or establish a physical operating window.
